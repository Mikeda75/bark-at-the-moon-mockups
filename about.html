The humans behind the howl

certified hands.

We're loud about the things that matter: rescue dogs, kind training, and doing this job right. Bark at the Moon started with one overlooked shelter dog, and everything about how we work traces back to her.

est. 2021
Founder & Co-Owner

Erica Piancone

VSA-CDT · Fear Free Certified® Professional · Trainer

It started in 2014 with Morticia — "Tish" — a senior pit bull who'd spent over a year in a shelter with nobody interested. Sparks flew. Tish opened the door to pit bull advocacy, to life with a reactive dog, and to the daily, patient work of positive reinforcement.

Years of dog walking, shelter volunteering, and rescue work later (including Villalobos Rescue Center in New Orleans), Erica enrolled at the Victoria Stilwell Academy and turned all of it into Bark at the Moon — the name being equal parts night-howl and Ozzy Osbourne tribute.

"Every dog deserves a handler who sees the superhero in them."
Co-Owner

Chaz Piancone

Current VSA Student · Trainer · U.S. Army Veteran

A Bradley Beach native, Chaz served six years in the Army across Signal and Military Intelligence battalions, and has spent years playing in bands. When he came home, he went after the other thing he actually loved: working with animals.

Now a Victoria Stilwell Academy student and full-time trainer and care professional, he brings the discipline of his service and none of the drill sergeant. He saves the volume for the stage. Nobody talks to dogs that way around here.
