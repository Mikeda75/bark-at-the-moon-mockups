What we work on

From "sit" to city streets.

  • 🤘 Basic manners: sit, stay, down, recall, polite greetings
  • 🤘 Leash skills: pulling, lunging, loose-leash walking that holds up outside
  • 🤘 Confidence building, especially for rescues and nervous dogs
  • 🤘 Mild behavioral issues: jumping, door-dashing, counter-surfing, that kind of thing
Real talk: we don't take on human-aggressive dogs, and we're not going to pretend otherwise. If that's your situation we'll point you to force-free specialists we trust. No charge, no judgment.
